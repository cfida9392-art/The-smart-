Academic Excellence

A comprehensive curriculum that balances knowledge, skills, and values.

Primary Wing

Classes 1–5 focused on foundational literacy, numeracy, creativity, and joyful learning through activity-based methods.

Middle Wing

Classes 6–8 introduce deeper subjects, scientific temper, and critical thinking with project-based learning.

Senior Wing

Classes 9–12 with specialized streams (Science, Commerce, Humanities) and career-oriented guidance.
