Curriculum

Our Academic Structure

A progressive curriculum that builds strong foundations and prepares students for higher education and life.

Primary (Classes 1–5)

Focus on literacy, numeracy, environmental awareness, arts, and physical education through play-based and activity-oriented methods. Emphasis on curiosity and confidence.

  • Language & Literature
  • Mathematics
  • Environmental Studies
  • Art, Music & Sports

Middle (Classes 6–8)

Introduction to specialized subjects, scientific inquiry, and project-based learning. Students develop analytical skills and independent learning habits.

  • Science & Technology
  • Social Sciences
  • Languages & Literature
  • Computer Applications

Senior (Classes 9–12)

Board-aligned curriculum with streams in Science, Commerce, and Humanities. Career counseling, competitive exam preparation, and research projects.

  • Science (PCM / PCB)
  • Commerce with Math
  • Humanities
  • Computer Science & Electives
